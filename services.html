SELECTED WORK

A selection of projects developed through my studies, internship, and external collaborations.

01 BOLD SCANDINAVIA - BRAND WORK

During a 10-week internship at Bold Scandinavia, part of The North Alliance (NoA), I worked across brand strategy, visual identity, and content for both national and international clients. I contributed to the development of a new visual identity for the medtech company C-RAD, working from early research and concept development through to graphic elements, color direction, and layout, presenting design directions to both the internal team and the client. I also designed visual communication for NoA, including an ESG report and social media content, and took part in the early concept phase of a Disney+ brand activation for the UEFA Women's Champions League final in Oslo. Across these projects, I learned to navigate feedback, iteration, and cross-team collaboration as a natural part of a dynamic design process, rather than a linear one.

02 BUSY BEING WELL - WEBSITE

Busy Being Well was our final project on Multimedia Design. The platform started as a podcast, and through this project, we saw an opportunity to design and code a website that could turn it into a more cohesive digital wellness universe and build a community around it. Through research, interviews, and benchmark analysis, we identified a need for a clearer, more trustworthy alternative to the fragmented and performative wellness content often found on social media.

Based on these insights, we designed and built a responsive website, developed a communication and activation strategy, and gave concrete recommendations for the podcasts social media presence, which led to an increase in likes and followers on TikTok and Instagram.

The project gave me hands-on experience in how design, content, and social strategy work together to build a brand's community.

03 DET GRØNLANDSKE HUS - REDESIGN

For my admission case for Creative Branding, I developed a full rebranding of Det Grønlandske Hus in Copenhagen, from research and brand strategy to visual identity and campaign. I created a new identity, "Kala Kulturhus København," and an opening campaign built around the line "Du er tættere på Grønland, end du tror." The project taught me how to translate research into a clear strategic direction and carry it through into a consistent visual and verbal identity.
