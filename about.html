ABOUT US

At LAgenzy, we believe that the strongest brands are built on a balance between experience and innovation. As a mother-daughter creative agency, we combine decades of industry expertise with a deep understanding of today’s fast-moving digital world. This unique dynamic allows us to create strategic, compelling, and visually powerful branding that resonates across generations.

With a background in creative direction, branding, and digital strategy, we know what it takes to cut through the noise. Our approach is thoughtful yet bold—grounded

LULU HENCKEL
CO-FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Lulu is a visionary creative leader with over 30 years of experience in brand development,marketing, and strategic innovation. Graduating with honors from Esmod in Paris, Lulu was once told that she was "too creative." She turned that challenge into her greatest asset, shaping her career around the belief that creativity, when applied strategically, is the key to unlocking transformative brand success.

From leading high-profile projects in Paris and New York, founding Youheshe.com in Scandinavia, and driving success as a creative director at Social Works, Lulu has consistently demonstrated her ability to build and optimize brands across every channel. Her expertise spans creative direction, branding, and marketing strategy, all fueled by an unwavering commitment to excellence. With her deep understanding of both creative and business strategy, Lulu has the vision to propel any brand to its next level of success.

ALVA HENCKEL
CO-FOUNDER & CREATIVE LEAD

Alva has been captivated by the visual and aesthetic world since childhood, growing up backstage at photoshoots and fashion shows alongside her mother. Choosing fashion over school whenever possible, she immersed herself in the industry from an early age. By 19, Alva had already built an impressive CV, earning her a spot on a Vogue shoot in Paris and since then led several photoshoots as stylist and creative director for brands among other like Helmstedt.

Over the past two years, she has worked as a freelance creative and stylist, collaborating closely with brands to enhance their social media presence, strengthen their relevance, and grow their audience through strategic, visually compelling content. As a Gen Z creative, she combines a deep understanding of the digital and social media landscape with an innovative vision and a natural and aesthetic flair for style in every project.
